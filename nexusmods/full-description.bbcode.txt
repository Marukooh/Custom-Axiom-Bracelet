[size=4][color=#D4A64A][b]Your bracelet. Your rules.[/b][/color][/size]
[size=3][color=#D4D4D8]
Kliff, Oongka and Damiane all wear the Axiom Bracelet, and it does next to nothing. [b]Custom Axiom Bracelet[/b] turns it into the piece of gear you want: one download, [b]29 options[/b] split into [b]7 cards[/b] in Definitive Mod Manager, and every option is its own checkbox. Want only the elemental resistances? Tick three boxes. Want the full god-tier bracelet? Tick them all.

[color=#FBBF24][b]Features[/b][/color]
[list]
[*][b]Pick buff by buff:[/b] every option is independent, so any combination works. No more choosing between a dozen pre-made editions.
[*][b]Grouped and readable:[/b] seven collapsible cards with plain names and a short description under every option.
[*][b]All three characters:[/b] each option applies to Kliff's, Oongka's and Damiane's Axiom Bracelet at once.
[*][b]Charting the Unknown ready:[/b] swim speed and breath buffs for the ocean, at the highest level the game defines. The breath options need the DLC and are skipped automatically without it.
[*][b]Quality of Life included:[/b] bigger stacks, more charges, shorter cooldowns and tougher gear, each one optional.
[*][b]Survives game updates:[/b] built on DMM's field-level format, which finds the right records by name instead of fixed byte offsets.
[/list]
[/color][/size]

[size=4][color=#D4A64A][b]A word on the Abyss effect limit[/b][/color][/size]
[size=3][color=#D4D4D8]Every bracelet buff counts as one Abyss effect, the same as the Abyss Gear socketed in your armor. Players report that going over [b]18 Abyss effects in total[/b] can leave the game stuck on the loading screen. Count your bracelet buffs together with your armor's Abyss Gear, and untick a few if you hit an endless load. Stats (Attack, Defense, speeds, critical rate), the two passives and the Quality of Life card are not Abyss effects.[/color][/size]

[size=4][color=#D4A64A][b]Requirements[/b][/color][/size]
[size=3][color=#D4D4D8]
[list]
[*]Crimson Desert [b]2.04.08[/b].

[*][b]Linux / Steam Deck:[/b] add [i]winmm=n,b[/i] to your launch options, e.g. [i]WINEDLLOVERRIDES="winmm=n,b" %command%[/i] (append it to your existing overrides if you already have some).
[/list]

[size=4][color=#D4A64A][b]Installation[/b][/color][/size]
[size=3][color=#D4D4D8]
[list=1]
[*]Use [b]Mod Manager Download[/b], or press [b]Import[/b] in DMM and pick the ZIP. You get seven cards named [i]Custom Axiom Bracelet 1 - Combat[/i] to [i]7 - Quality of Life[/i].
[*]Tick the options you want in each card, then press [b]Mount[/b].
[/list]
To change your build, tick or untick options and Mount again. To uninstall, untick everything and Mount (or Unmount). Your save stays loadable.
[/color][/size]

[size=4][color=#D4A64A][b]Compatibility[/b][/color][/size]
[size=3][color=#D4D4D8]Works alongside other DMM mods. Mods that edit the Axiom Bracelet, or the stack size, charges, cooldowns or durability of the same items, overlap with the matching options: the later mod in your load order wins. Do not use it together with other Axiom Bracelet buff mods.[/color][/size]
